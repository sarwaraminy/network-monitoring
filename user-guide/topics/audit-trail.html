Audit trail

Who deleted, changed or redirected something.

The audit trail answers questions that no other screen can: who turned delivery off, who deleted that finding, when did the recipients change. It is append-only and never pruned, and the append-only property is enforced by the database rather than by the application — an entry cannot be edited or removed, including by an administrator, and including from the Ad Hoc Query console.

Each entry is written in the same transaction as the act it records, so an action that succeeded is on the trail and an action that failed is not.

Reading it

ColumnHolds
WhenThe time of the act, in your timezone.
WhoThe account that performed it.
WhatThe action in words, with its internal name beneath — useful when asking for support.
WhichThe thing acted on, where one identifier describes it.
DetailWhat distinguishes this entry from the next — for a settings change, the list of fields that moved.

The Action control at the top right filters to one kind of event.

Note

Values are deliberately absent from the Detail column for settings changes. A change records that webhookUrl and emailPassword were altered, never what they were altered to — otherwise this table would be a second place to read credentials, one that cannot be pruned and that every administrator can read in full.

Who can use this

Administrators only. The entry is hidden from the navigation panel for other accounts, and the underlying address refuses them too.
